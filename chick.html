Introduction to Poultry Farming in Kenya

What is Poultry Farming?

Poultry farming is the practice of raising domesticated birds such as chickens, ducks, turkeys, and geese for the purpose of producing meat, eggs, and other products. In Kenya, poultry farming is a significant agricultural activity that contributes to food security and income generation.

There are various types of poultry farming, including broiler farming for meat production and layer farming for egg production. Each type requires specific management practices to ensure the health and productivity of the birds.

Overall, poultry farming is a vital component of Kenya's agricultural sector, providing employment opportunities and contributing to the country's economic growth.

Benefits of Poultry Farming

Poultry farming offers numerous benefits, including:

  • Source of high-quality protein through meat and eggs.
  • Income generation for farmers and their families.
  • Employment opportunities in rural areas.
  • Utilization of poultry manure as a natural fertilizer for crops.
  • Contribution to food security by providing affordable protein sources.

Challenges in Poultry Farming

Despite its benefits, poultry farming in Kenya faces several challenges, including:

  • Disease outbreaks that can lead to significant losses.
  • High costs of feed and other inputs.
  • Limited access to veterinary services and extension support.
  • Market fluctuations affecting the prices of poultry products.
  • Environmental concerns related to waste management and pollution.
